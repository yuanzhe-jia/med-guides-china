# Healthcare Costs in Beijing: What to Expect

One of the biggest questions international patients have is: *how much will this cost?* The good news is that for most procedures, you'll find significant savings compared to Western countries — without compromising on quality.

## The Big Picture: Cost Comparison

| Procedure | Beijing (Int'l Dept) | United States | United Kingdom (Private) |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------|-------------------------|
| Consultation (specialist) | $50 - $300 | $300 - $800 | £150 - £400 |
| MRI Scan | $120 - $350 | $1,000 - $5,000 | £300 - £800 |
| Knee Replacement (one side) | $8,000 - $18,000 | $35,000 - $70,000 | £10,000 - £20,000 |
| Coronary Angioplasty | $5,000 - $12,000 | $30,000 - $80,000 | £6,000 - £12,000 |
| Normal Delivery | $3,000 - $8,000 | $10,000 - $30,000 | £3,000 - £7,000 |

*Note: These are approximate ranges. Actual costs depend on hospital, complexity, and length of stay.*

## What Affects the Price?

### 1. Hospital Type
- **Public hospital international departments**: Mid-range prices, excellent quality. The best value for most people.
- **Premium private hospitals** (e.g., United Family): Higher prices (2-3x public international), but more Western-style amenities and convenience.
- **Public general departments**: Lowest prices, but typically Chinese-only service and longer waits. Not recommended unless you speak fluent Chinese.

### 2. Procedure Complexity
Routine procedures fall into predictable ranges. Complex cases (e.g., revision surgery, rare conditions) may cost more due to additional tests, longer hospital stays, or specialist teams.

### 3. Room Type
Hospital rooms come in several tiers:
- **Shared ward**: Most affordable, 2-4 patients per room
- **Private room**: Standard in international departments, your own room with bathroom
- **VIP/suite**: Luxury options available at some hospitals

## Getting an Accurate Quote

For any planned procedure, you can get a cost estimate before you travel. Here's how:

1. **Contact the hospital's international department** by phone or email
2. **Send your medical records** (test results, imaging reports, doctor's notes)
3. **Ask for a detailed cost estimate** including:
   - Surgeon and anesthesiologist fees
   - Hospital room and meals
   - Pre-operative tests
   - Medications
   - Expected follow-up visits

Most international departments will provide a written quote within 3-5 business days.

## Insurance & Payment

### Direct Billing
If you have international health insurance with a plan that covers China, you may be eligible for direct billing. This means:
- You show your insurance card at registration
- The hospital bills your insurance company directly
- You only pay for any uncovered services out of pocket

Major providers with direct billing in Beijing include Aetna, Cigna, MSH, Allianz, Bupa, and AIA.

### Paying Out of Pocket
If you're paying directly, hospitals accept:
- **Cash** (RMB preferred, some accept USD/EUR)
- **Credit/debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay)
- **Mobile payment** (WeChat Pay, Alipay — if you have a Chinese bank account)

**Always ask for a bilingual receipt** (中英文发票) for insurance reimbursement.
