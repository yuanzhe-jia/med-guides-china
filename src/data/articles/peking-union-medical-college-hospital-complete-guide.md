# Peking Union Medical College Hospital: Complete Guide for International Patients

Peking Union Medical College Hospital (PUMCH) — often called "Xiehe" by locals — is China's most prestigious hospital. Founded in 1921 by the Rockefeller Foundation, it has set the standard for Chinese medicine for over a century.

## Why Choose PUMCH?

- **China's #1 ranked hospital** for overall clinical excellence
- **Strongest across most specialties** — oncology, rheumatology, obstetrics, endocrinology, allergy, and more
- **Decades of experience** serving international patients from 180+ countries
- **Multidisciplinary teams** for complex cases
- **Direct billing with most major insurers**

## The International Medical Service Department

The International Medical Service (IMS) is the part of PUMCH specifically for foreign patients. Here's what you get:

- **English-speaking doctors and nurses**
- **Dedicated international patient coordinators**
- **Shorter wait times** than the general hospital
- **Private consultation rooms**
- **Direct insurance billing**
- **Assistance with medical records and certificates**

The IMS is located in a separate building on the PUMCH campus, with its own entrance and registration desk.

## How to Book an Appointment

### By Phone (Recommended)
Call the International Medical Service hotline: **+86-10-6915-6699**

Operating hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

### What You'll Need
- Your passport number
- Brief description of your condition
- Preferred appointment date/time
- Any relevant medical records (can be emailed)

### Appointment Types
- **General outpatient**: Available Monday-Friday, usually bookable 2-3 days in advance
- **Specialist consultation**: Popular specialists may book 1-2 weeks out
- **Second opinion**: You can specifically request a second opinion appointment

Show the driver this: *"协和医院国际医疗部，东城区帅府园1号"*

From Sanlitun: ~15-25 minutes depending on traffic
From the airport: ~45-60 minutes

## What to Expect on Your Visit

1. **Arrive 20 minutes early** for registration
2. **Bring your passport** — you'll need it to create your medical record
3. **Show your insurance card** if using direct billing
4. **A patient coordinator** will guide you through the process
5. **Consultation time** is usually 15-30 minutes, longer for new patients
6. **Tests and imaging** can often be done the same day

## Top Specialties at PUMCH

- **Rheumatology**: China's strongest, with a global reputation for autoimmune diseases
- **Obstetrics & Gynecology**: High-risk pregnancies, reproductive endocrinology
- **Endocrinology**: Diabetes, thyroid disorders, pituitary diseases
- **Allergy & Immunology**: Food allergies, asthma, immune disorders
- **Oncology**: Multidisciplinary cancer care with access to clinical trials
- **Dermatology**: Complex skin conditions, cosmetic dermatology
